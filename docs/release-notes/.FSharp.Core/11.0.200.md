### Fixed

* Fix LINQ providers failing on, or evaluating on the client, queries that end by consuming the tuples or records a query produces: `exists`, `all`, `sumBy`, `averageBy`, `minBy`, `maxBy` and `count` after two `for`s, a `join` or a tuple or record `select` now work on the query's own elements instead of on tuples and records rebuilt at the end of the query.
* Fix struct tuples in query expressions and in lambdas converted to LINQ expression trees: selecting one no longer fails with "unreachable", and taking one apart no longer fails with a tuple index out of range. ([Issue #15522](https://github.com/dotnet/fsharp/issues/15522))
