### Fixed

* Fix LINQ providers failing on a `groupBy` or `groupValBy` over tuples or records followed by `select (g.Key, g.Count())`, `g.Sum(..)`, `g.Average(..)` and similar: FSharp.Core no longer rebuilds each group as a `Grouping` of tuples in the middle of the query, and no longer re-projects every element after a `join` or a `for`. ([Issue #47](https://github.com/dotnet/fsharp/issues/47))

* Fix struct tuples in query expressions and in lambdas converted to LINQ expression trees: selecting one no longer fails with "unreachable", and taking one apart no longer fails with a tuple index out of range. ([Issue #15522](https://github.com/dotnet/fsharp/issues/15522))
